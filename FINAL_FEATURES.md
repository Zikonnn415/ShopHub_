## 🚀 How to Use Each Feature

### **Search Products:**
1. Use navbar search bar
2. Or use search in Product List page
3. Results filter in real-time

### **Filter Products:**
1. Go to Product List page
2. Use sidebar filters:
   - Select category
   - Adjust price range
   - Click "Reset Filters" to clear

### **Sort Products:**
1. Use dropdown in Product List
2. Choose sorting option
3. Products reorder automatically

### **Add to Cart:**
1. Click "Add to Cart" on any product
2. Must be logged in
3. Toast notification confirms

### **Manage Cart:**
1. Click cart icon in navbar
2. Use +/- buttons for quantity
3. Apply promo codes (SAVE10, SAVE20)
4. Click "Proceed to Checkout"

### **Use Dashboard:**
1. Login to your account
2. View statistics
3. Click quick action cards
4. Check recent activity
